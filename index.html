Per-Project Per-Agency Spend Heatmap

Each cell shows the cumulative dollars billed for that project where that agency appeared on the lobbying firm's quarterly disclosure. Source: Allen Matkins Form 34 (Q1 2025 - Q1 2026) and Marathon Communications Form 34 (Q2 2025 - Q1 2026). Methodology: for each quarter the agency was listed on the firm's filing for that matter, the full matter fee is added to the agency total. Quarters listed as "No Agencies Lobbied" with a non-zero fee are excluded from any agency cell (footnote at row level).

Hide rows:

Note on Q4 2025 Marathon entries: Marathon's Q4 2025 Form 34 listed an identical 10-agency string ("Mayor; City Council; Building & Safety; Fire (LAFD); Harbor (POLA); Neighborhood Councils; Planning, City; Police (LAPD); Transportation (LADOT); Water & Power (DWP)") on every active matter including Academy Museum Gala and several $0-fee clients. This pattern does not appear in Q1 2025, Q2 2025, Q3 2025, or Q1 2026 Marathon filings and is flagged in the data. The dollars Q4 2025 contributes to LADWP and other agencies in the Marathon rows below come from that boilerplate.
