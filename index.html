Westlake Lobbying Analysis

Two Westlake parcels (2727 W. 6th Street and 525 S. Virgil Avenue). Sources: LA City Ethics Commission Form 31, 33, 34 filings; LA County Assessor APN records; SEC EDGAR (Digital Realty Trust, L.P. Form 10-K Exhibit 21.2, FY ending Dec. 31, 2025).

This map shows the chain of ownership and the flow of money.

Five Westlake properties share the same law firm/lobbyist, Allen Matkins, and another lobbying firm, Marathon Communications.

Allen Matkins handles a data center at 600 W. 7th Street for Digital Realty (LAX10) and a planned data center at 727 S. Grand. They’re a commercial real estate law firm with 14 registered lobbyists, currently expanding their data center clients and data center team.

Marathon Communications is paid by NextEra Energy, the largest supplier of data center power in the US; Bechtel Corporation, which builds data centers and the power plants that run them; and Arevon, an energy company that supplies data centers with their electricity.

Digital Realty owns the pipeline. Digital Realty owns the LAX Transpacific Cable Landing Project, identified in Exhibit 21.2 to Digital Realty Trust, L.P.’s Form 10-K for fiscal year ending December 31, 2025. The pipeline runs from downtown LA through Westlake to the beach, where it connects to submarine cables to the world.

Thomas Faraut, a commercial real estate developer at Crescent Heights, has been attending Neighborhood Council meetings in support of the “self storage” projects without disclosing his Crescent Heights affiliation. Several callers spoke immediately afterward in support of the storage center.

When complete, the pipeline would have the capacity to support a cluster of data centers larger than any in the world. Ten to thirty times the output of Las Vegas.

The owner or owners of the five Westlake properties that sit on the pipeline’s path remains undisclosed.
