Two Westlake parcels (2727 W. 6th Street and 525 S. Virgil Avenue). Sources: LA City Ethics Commission Form 34 quarterlies (Allen Matkins Q1 2025 - Q1 2026; Marathon Communications Q2 2025 - Q1 2026); LA County Assessor APN records.

Same Team, Two Data Centers, One Pipeline, Five Parcels

Five Westlake properties share the same law firm, Allen Matkins, and the same lobbying firm, Marathon Communications.

Allen Matkins handles a data center at 600 W. 7th Street for Digital Realty (LAX10) and a planned data center at 727 S. Grand. They're a commercial real estate law firm with 14 registered lobbyists, currently expanding their data center clients and data center team.

Marathon Communications is paid by
NextEra Energy, the largest supplier of data center power in the US;
Bechtel Corporation, which builds data centers and the power plants that run them; and
Arevon, an energy company that supplies data centers with their electricity.

M Strategic Communications — also paid by Digital Realty — has been lobbying for the transpacific data-line pipeline that connects downtown to the Pacific. When complete, the pipeline would have the capacity to support a cluster of data centers larger than any in the world. Ten to thirty times the output of Las Vegas.

The end client, who owns all five Westlake properties that sit on the pipeline, remains undisclosed.
